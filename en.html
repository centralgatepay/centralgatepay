    How it works

    From click to cash, in four steps

    Collect

    Your customer pays through our checkout, your own form or a payment link. Card details are tokenized before they leave the browser.

    Decide

    The risk engine scores the transaction in real time and applies your rules, with 3-D Secure only where it is justified.

    Route

    We pick the acquirer with the best approval odds and cost for that card, and retry elsewhere when a soft decline comes back.

    Settle

    Funds land in your balance with a full audit trail, then move to your bank account on the schedule you choose.

    Dashboard

    Every transaction, fully explained

    Stop guessing why a payment failed. CentralGatePay shows the full lifecycle of each transaction, from the first authorization attempt to the line on your bank statement.

    Decline intelligence

    Group failures by bank, BIN and reason code, and recover the ones you actually can.

    Real-time reconciliation

    Payouts are matched to transactions automatically and exported to 1C or your accounting stack.

    Chargeback workspace

    Receive disputes, attach evidence and track the outcome without leaving the dashboard.

    FAQ

    Questions we hear before every integration

    Didn't find your answer? Message us on Telegram and we'll reply within 15 minutes.

    How long does it take to go live?

    Most businesses complete onboarding and take their first payments within one or two business days. Technical integration with our SDKs usually takes a day, and we run the first transactions with you in the sandbox before issuing production keys.

    Which payment methods can I accept?

    Mir cards, Visa and Mastercard issued by Russian banks, SBP, SberPay, T-Pay, Mir Pay and pay-in-installments. Every method settles into the same balance.

    When is the money settled?

    Card volume settles on the next business day (D+1). Other methods follow the schedule of the underlying payment system. Transfers to your bank account can run daily, weekly or on demand.

    Do I need my own cash register?

    No. CentralGatePay's cloud cash register issues 54-FZ receipts and sends them to the customer by email or SMS.

    Does my company need to be PCI DSS compliant?

    If you use our hosted checkout or widget, card data never touches your servers, so your requirements stay minimal. If you collect card data directly, we'll help you map the requirements that apply.

    How does pricing work?

    You pay only on successful transactions, with no setup fee and no monthly minimum. Your rate depends on volume, method mix and risk profile, and we put it in writing before you sign.

    What happens when a customer disputes a charge?

    You're notified the moment the dispute opens, with the reason code and the deadline. Evidence is submitted from the dashboard or via API, and the case is tracked until the final decision.
